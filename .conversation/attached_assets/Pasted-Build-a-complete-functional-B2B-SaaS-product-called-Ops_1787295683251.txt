==================================================
PRODUCT
==================================================

OpsPilot AI helps customer-support teams:

- Manage customers
- Manage support tickets
- Assign tickets to agents/teams
- Manage conversations and internal notes
- Search an internal knowledge base
- Summarize tickets with AI
- Classify tickets with AI

- Suggest customer responses
- Recommend escalation
- Recommend next actions
- Find similar tickets
- Track SLAs
- Monitor team performance
- View analytics
- Maintain audit logs
- Manage users, teams and permissions
